The short version

Team Feed runs entirely inside Atlassian's Forge platform. It has no servers of its own, no third-party analytics, no advertising, no AI services, and nothing is ever sold. What you post stays in your Confluence site's app storage.

What the app stores

  • Feed settings, membership, publication permissions and account identifiers.
  • Posts, comments and their revision history, with author attribution.
  • Source links, titles, selected descriptions and versions used to publish document updates and avoid duplicates.
  • Your private reading state (seen, saved, following, reading position). Other users and exports never see it.
  • Publisher signing keys (in Forge secret storage) and minimal technical records for security and recovery.

Current display names are fetched live from Confluence and not stored. Original documents stay in Confluence or your Git repository; text that users submit may itself identify people. Your browser remembers only the last opened feed and a visit identifier.

Roles

Your organization (the Confluence customer) controls workspace data; the provider processes it on your instructions under the data-processing agreement, which forms part of the app's terms. For support correspondence and business administration the provider is responsible directly. Data is hosted by Atlassian on the Forge platform; eligible stored data follows your site's data residency, while compute can run elsewhere (Forge residency limits).

Retention

  • Posts default to a 60-day lifetime, then archive; administrators can set 1–3,650 days and choose archive or deletion. Deleting a post removes its discussion and associated reading state.
  • Account and configuration records are kept while the installation uses them and are removed with feed deletion or uninstall. Atlassian removes remaining app data about 28 days after uninstall.
  • Technical publication/deduplication/security records are kept no longer than the installation and are removed with it.
  • Support email is handled personally by the provider through Hostpoint (Swiss-hosted mail) and deleted within 12 months after a case closes; Hostpoint's own backups expire within at most 180 days. Records needed for a specific legal obligation or dispute may be kept longer, restricted to that purpose.

See Data and retention for export and uninstall limits.

Your rights

For access, correction or deletion of your data, contact us with your site's name — do not send credentials or private documents initially. Requests are verified with your site's administrator before anything is changed. Removing personal state or attribution does not automatically rewrite shared text that identifies someone; that is reviewed case by case.

Reference: FDPIC guidance on privacy notices.
