Quick reference

The short version: three steps to get going, then one line per setting. The getting started guide has the details.

Get going in three steps

  1. Open the app. In Confluence: Apps → Catch-Up.
  2. Create a feed. Select Create a shared feed, name it, choose visibility, and add people under ⚙ Feed settings → Access.
  3. Share a page. On any published page: ••• → Apps → Share to Feed (or the page banner). Pick the feed, write one or two sentences, Share update. Done — optionally tick Track future changes on the confirmation screen.

Common questions

Why can't someone see a feed? They need feed access (Feed settings → Access) and Confluence access to the linked pages. Following alone grants nothing.

Why isn't tracking automatic after sharing? Tracking publishes future page versions on its own, so it always asks you to confirm a preview of what it will do — one extra click, on purpose.

How fast do tracked edits show up? Edits are grouped after about five quiet minutes (at most fifteen) so one editing session becomes one post, not ten.

Did I lose my place? No — each person keeps a private reading position, and a New divider marks where you left off. Opening a feed never marks everything as seen.

Where does my data live? Entirely inside Atlassian (Forge storage on your own site). No external servers, no analytics, no AI. Details on the data & retention page.
