The service

Team Feed provides shared document updates and discussion within Confluence Cloud, with optional Azure DevOps and GitHub publishers. Installation requires an authorized Confluence administrator. Customers control membership, publication permissions and sources; publishers must have permission to share submitted content. Protect publisher signing keys and review recipients before enabling a publisher.

Confluence posts require source access. Repository publication shares approved metadata with feed members without checking individual repository membership. Content remains its rights holder's property; the provider processes it only to provide, secure and support the agreed service.

Product limits

Original documents stay in Confluence/Git. Team Feed is not a backup: feed export is limited to 100 posts/1 MB, excludes private reading state and has no restore function. "Seen" is not acknowledgement. Post lifetime defaults to 60 days, then archive; administrators choose 1–3,650 days and archive or deletion. Archive retains data. See Data and retention.
