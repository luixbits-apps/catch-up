Team Feed support

Email contact@luizperren.dev for help with Team Feed. For a missing feed or document, first ask its owner to check your access.

Include what you tried, what happened, the error text, the approximate time and the app version when available. A redacted screenshot or repository run ID can help. Do not send signing keys, API tokens, full logs or private document contents.

For suspected security issues, use the same address with Team Feed security in the subject. Describe the affected action and impact without exploiting other users' data or publishing sensitive details. For account-data requests and recovery after uninstall, see Data and retention.

Before retrying an update

  • Confluence edits are grouped after five quiet minutes, up to a fifteen-minute grouping window. Refresh sources does not accelerate publication.
  • For repository updates, check the pipeline run summary and keep the successful checkpoint artifact. Do not change the starting commit to bypass a recovery error.
  • If automatic updates are paused, ask the owner to inspect the source status and app entitlement before rerunning jobs. Existing source documents stay intact.

Support policy

The ordinary initial response target is three business days, Monday–Friday excluding public holidays in Schwyz, Switzerland (Europe/Zurich). This is a target, not a guaranteed response or resolution time. No general 24/7 customer service is offered. Marketplace obligations take precedence: Atlassian-designated critical requests receive a response within 24 hours, and other requests within five business days (Marketplace Partner Agreement, section 6.3).

Support continues while Team Feed is offered, and existing paid service and support obligations are honored. For a planned discontinuation, the aim is at least 90 days' notice and clear information about customer data and options. Necessary security or legal action may happen sooner; existing paid obligations and mandatory rights remain.

Support mail is handled personally by the provider through Hostpoint. Correspondence is deleted from active records within 12 months after a request closes, except records needed for a specific legal obligation or dispute; those are restricted to that purpose and removed when the need ends. Mail-provider backups follow their separate retention policy.
